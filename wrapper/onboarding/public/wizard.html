Connect Gmail

ArmorClaw reads your inbox and drafts replies. You confirm before anything is sent.

App passwords: App passwords give ArmorClaw complete access to your Gmail inbox — the same access as your password. Only connect an account you're comfortable sharing. Your app password is stored in your Mac's secure keychain and never transmitted anywhere except Gmail's servers.

How to set up:

  1. In your Google Account, go to Security → App passwords → myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
  2. Create a new app password. Name it "ArmorClaw".
  3. Paste the 16-character password below.
