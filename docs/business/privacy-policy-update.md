### Third-party services and AI providers

**Payment processing:** Stripe, Inc. processes payments on our behalf.
Their privacy policy is available at stripe.com/privacy.

**AI model providers:** ArmorClaw works with three types of AI providers.
Your choice of provider determines where your conversations are processed:

LOCAL (fully private): If you use Ollama, all AI processing happens
entirely on your computer. No conversation data leaves your machine.
This is the most private option.

CLOUD (processed by third party): If you use Anthropic (Claude) or
OpenAI (ChatGPT), your conversations are sent to and processed by those
companies on their servers, under their own privacy policies and terms
of service. Shuriken Labs does not intercept, store, or have access to
these conversations - they go directly from your computer to the
provider you chose.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of whichever AI provider you
select. Anthropic's policy is at anthropic.com/privacy. OpenAI's policy
is at openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.

If keeping your conversations entirely on your computer is important to
you, choose Ollama during setup.
