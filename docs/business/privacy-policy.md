## Shuriken Labs LLC

### The short version

Shuriken Labs does not collect, store, or have access to your conversations,
files, or agent activity. Everything ArmorClaw does happens on your computer.
Where your data goes beyond that depends on which AI provider you choose and
what third-party components you install - this policy explains exactly what
that means.

---

### What Shuriken Labs collects

When you subscribe to ArmorClaw, we collect your name and email address for
account management and billing purposes. That is the extent of what we collect.

Payment processing is handled by Stripe, Inc. We receive confirmation that a
payment was made and your billing details (name, email, last four digits of
your card). We do not store full payment card numbers - Stripe handles this
directly under their own privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy.

We collect no data about how you use ArmorClaw, what you ask your agent, what
files you access, or what actions your agent takes. This information exists
only on your computer.

---

### Your AI provider and where conversations go

ArmorClaw works with three types of AI providers. Your choice determines where
your conversations are processed. We are clear about this because it matters.

**Ollama (local — fully private)**
If you use Ollama, all AI processing happens entirely on your computer using
a model you downloaded. No conversation data leaves your machine under any
circumstances. This is the most private option available.

**Anthropic or OpenAI (cloud — processed by a third party)**
If you use Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI (ChatGPT), your conversations are
sent to and processed by those companies on their servers. This happens
directly between your computer and their servers - Shuriken Labs does not
intercept, store, or have access to these conversations. However, they are
subject to those companies' privacy policies and terms of service.

Anthropic's privacy policy: anthropic.com/privacy
OpenAI's privacy policy: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy

We recommend reading those policies before choosing a cloud provider. If
keeping your conversations entirely on your computer is a requirement,
choose Ollama.
