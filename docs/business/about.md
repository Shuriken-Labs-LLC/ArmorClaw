# About ArmorClaw

ArmorClaw is a personal AI assistant built for people who take their privacy seriously.

We built ArmorClaw because we believe the most useful AI tool is one you can actually trust. Not trust because someone promised you they protect your data - trust because your data never leaves your computer in the first place.

ArmorClaw runs entirely on your machine. Your conversations, your files, your client information - none of it passes through our servers. We don't have access to it. We never will. When you ask your agent to research a contact or draft a follow-up, that work happens locally, and you see every step before it executes.

We designed it for the professional who isn't a developer but is serious about their work. Real estate agents managing dozens of active relationships. Financial advisors who can't afford a data breach. Freelancers who wear every hat and need leverage without sacrificing control.

The daily experience is simple: your agent checks in every morning, tells you what needs attention, and waits for your direction. You approve what matters. You undo what doesn't land right. You see everything in a clean dashboard that doesn't require a manual to read.

ArmorClaw is built and maintained by Shuriken Labs LLC, a software company based in Milan, Michigan. We're a small team building tools we use ourselves.
