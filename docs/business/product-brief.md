# ArmorClaw Product Brief

## Shuriken Labs LLC

**Your AI assistant. Your computer. Your control.**

ArmorClaw is a secure, user-friendly AI assistant that runs entirely on your own computer. It connects to the AI model of your choice and handles the repetitive tasks that eat your day - triaging messages, researching contacts, managing your schedule, and staying on top of your pipeline - without sending your data anywhere you didn't authorize.

Most AI tools require you to trust a cloud service with your conversations, your contacts, and your work. ArmorClaw is different. Everything runs locally. Your data stays on your machine. You see exactly what the agent does before it does it, and you can undo it if you change your mind.

**Who it's for:** Freelancers, real estate agents, independent financial advisors, and small business owners who want the productivity benefits of AI without handing their client relationships and business data to a third party.

**What it does out of the box:** Communicates with you via Telegram and a desktop chat window, manages files in a secure sandbox on your computer, integrates with your CRM, automates browser tasks, and runs scheduled recipes for recurring workflows. Everything is visible in a plain-English dashboard. Nothing happens without your awareness.

**AI provider options:**

- Ollama (local): All processing stays on your computer. Fully private. No API costs.
- Anthropic (Claude): Powerful cloud AI. Conversations processed by Anthropic.
- OpenAI (ChatGPT): Powerful cloud AI. Conversations processed by OpenAI.
  Users choose their provider during setup. The choice is permanent and explained clearly.

**What makes it different:** It's the only AI assistant that runs on your hardware, logs every action in a human-readable audit trail, and requires your approval before anything irreversible happens. Security that used to require an IT department, packaged for someone who has never used a terminal.

**Pricing:** Free for the first month. $19.99/month thereafter. Bring your own API key from Anthropic or OpenAI, or run Ollama locally at no cost.
