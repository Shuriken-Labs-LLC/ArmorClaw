We built this because we couldn't find it

Every AI tool we tried wanted us to hand over our conversations, our contacts, our client notes. That's a non-starter if you're managing real relationships and real money.

ArmorClaw runs entirely on your machine. When you ask it to draft a follow-up or research a contact, that work happens locally. You see every step before it executes. You approve what matters. You undo what doesn't land right.

We designed it for the professional who isn't a developer but takes their work seriously. Real estate agents managing dozens of active relationships. Financial advisors who can't afford a data breach. Freelancers who wear every hat and need leverage without sacrificing control.

What it does for you

Morning briefing

Every morning, ArmorClaw checks in on Telegram with what happened yesterday, what's on your calendar today, and what needs your attention. Like a good assistant, it doesn't wait to be asked.

Everything visible

A plain-English dashboard shows every action the agent takes, every permission it uses, and every dollar it spends on AI. Nothing happens in the background without your awareness.

Approval before action

Before ArmorClaw sends an email or does anything irreversible, it asks you first. And if something doesn't look right, you have 60 seconds to undo it with one tap.

Your budget, your limit

Set a monthly spending cap on AI usage. ArmorClaw tracks every token and stops before you go over. No surprise bills. If you run a local model, your cost is zero.

You choose where your conversations go
